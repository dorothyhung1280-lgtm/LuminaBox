LuminaBox is a bilingual learning kit and digital platform designed to help children gain confidence in English while staying connected to their schools, families, and communities.

Created by Bridge to English, a student-led charity with over 200 volunteers, LuminaBox makes learning fun, practical, and supportive. Families can use it at home with ease, while children enjoy gamified missions, rewards, and mentorship.
