LuminaBox — Bilingual SEN Learning Support Kit LuminaBox 光啟盒 — 雙語 SEN 特殊學習需要輔助套裝

LuminaBox is a bilingual learning kit and digital platform designed to help children gain confidence in English while staying connected to their schools, families, and communities. LuminaBox 光啟盒是一套結合實體教材與線上 AI 平台的雙語學習套裝，旨在協助兒童建立英語自信，並深化與學校、家庭及社區的連結。

Created by Bridge to English, a student-led charity with over 200 volunteers, LuminaBox makes learning fun, practical, and supportive. Families can use it at home with ease, while children enjoy gamified missions, rewards, and mentorship. 本計畫由擁有超過 200 名導師的學生慈善機構「Bridge to English」創立，將實體工作簿與智能 AI 相結合，提供有趣且無負擔的學習體驗。
