Transforming English Learning into an Unstoppable Adventure.

LuminaBox (啟迪語言連繫箱) is a high-impact physical learning kit designed for all primary school children, specifically tailored to ensure learning is seamless and approachable for underprivileged students and neurodivergent/SEN learners. For just HKD $20, LuminaBox turns daunting grammar and vocabulary into bite-sized physical tasks paired with an interactive digital reward portal!

What's Inside Every LuminaBox?

Each kit represents 1 Month Chapter packed with structured visual tools designed to prevent overwhelm:

  • 4 Weekly Targeted Activity Sheets (Speak, Read, Write, Connect)
  • 4-Digit Physical Code Dial Cipher Card
  • Dyslexia-Friendly Social Interaction Flashcards
  • Bridge2English Volunteer Support Pass
Structure That Works

1 Month Chapter • 4 Focused Weekly Targets

Instead of thick, intimidating textbooks, kids conquer one bite-sized target per week through simple yet powerful interactive Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQs).

W1

Week 1: SPEAK 🗣️

Simple MCQ phonics & voice practice prompts. Building oral confidence in a low-pressure environment.

W2

Week 2: READ 📖

Visual card matching and decoding everyday notices or signs into simplified, digestible chunks.

W3

Week 3: WRITE ✍️

Powerful MCQ sentence construction and vocabulary detective puzzles that make grammar feel like solving a mystery.

W4

Week 4: CONNECT 💬

Applying learning to real life! Connect with Bridge2English mentors via chat or Zoom to practice social conversations.

Thoughtful Access

Designed to Make Learning Easier for Everyone

We don't isolate learners with labels. We incorporate universal design features that naturally support neurodivergent students and underprivileged youth who lack costly tutoring resources.

✨

Bite-Sized MCQ Mechanics

Long-form writing tests induce anxiety. Our streamlined Multiple Choice Questions allow students to build instant momentum and focus on core mastery.

👀

Sensory & Dyslexia Readability

Soft pastels (sky blue, calming yellow), specialized font toggles, and generous letter-spacing remove visual stress for neurodivergent learners.

💎

Gamified Reward Loop

Completing weekly exercises yields Lumina Shards, unlocking adorable avatar companions and stage badges to spark intrinsic interest in English.
