LuminaBox — Monthly 4-Pillar Bilingual Learning Kit LuminaBox 光啟盒 — 每月四大核心雙語學習輔助套裝

LuminaBox is a monthly bilingual learning kit and digital platform designed to build English confidence for children through structured weekly goals. Each month delivers four distinct thematic booklets focusing on Speak, Read, Write, and Connect. LuminaBox 光啟盒是一套結合實體教材與線上 AI 平台的雙語學習套裝。每月按週規劃四本主題冊，分別著重於「口說 (Speak)」、「閱讀 (Read)」、「寫作 (Write)」與「社區連結 (Connect)」，協助兒童全方位建立英語自信。

Created by Bridge to English, a student-led charity with over 200 volunteers, LuminaBox makes learning structured, actionable, and engaging. Children complete daily exercises across 5 weekdays, calculate a 5-digit Lumina Code, and enter it into LuminaPortal AI to earn Lumina Shards, upgrade companions, and access live Zoom mentorship! 本計畫由擁有超過 200 名導師的學生慈善機構「Bridge to English」創立。學童完成工作天微任務計算出 5 位數「Lumina 密碼」，即可登入 LuminaPortal AI 賺取 Lumina Shards 晶石，升級 AI 寵物並預約 1對1 Zoom 導師！

What's Inside Your LuminaBox? LuminaBox 光啟盒包含什麼？

📚

4 Thematic Booklets 4 本單週主題小冊

Booklets for Speak, Read, Write, and Connect, featuring dyslexia-friendly fonts and sensory-rich layouts. 針對「說、讀、寫、社區連結」設計的單週冊，採用讀寫障礙友善字體與多感官排版。

🔢

5-Digit Lumina Card 5 位數 Lumina 算式卡

Daily calculation card to log weekly progress, sum daily scores, and derive your Lumina Code for online rewards. 每日進度記錄卡，相加當天數值登入得出當週專屬解密金鑰。

📹

Zoom Mentorship Pass Zoom 導師一鍵預約卡

Connect 1-to-1 with student volunteers from Bridge to English for personalized Zoom oral and homework guidance. 線上一鍵預約 Bridge to English 導師，提供專屬 1對1 Zoom 語音與作業答疑。

🧩

Tactile Learning Accessories 觸感互動學習配件

Includes visual prompt cards, reward stickers, and community task badges for hands-on sensory learners. 包含視覺提示卡、獎勵貼紙與社區任務徽章，激發孩子觸感學習興趣。

Special Educational Needs (SEN) Tailored 特殊教育需要 (SEN) 專屬設計

Inclusive Design for Every Unique Learner 專為多元學習者打造的無障礙體驗

LuminaBox and LuminaPortal incorporate research-backed tools to support Dyslexia, ADHD, and neurodivergent learning patterns. 結合學術研究驗證的教學工具，全面支援讀寫障礙、專注力不足 (ADHD) 及多元學習需求的學童。

🔤

Dyslexia-Friendly Fonts 讀寫障礙友善字體

Built-in Lexend typography toggle with wide letter-spacing to prevent visual crowding and reading fatigue. 一鍵開啟 Lexend 專用字體，加寬字距與行距，大幅降低閱讀視覺壓力與字符混淆。

🎨

Soft Pastel High-Contrast Tint 柔和高對比背景調色

Warm cream-yellow backgrounds eliminate harsh screen glare and decrease visual stress during reading sessions. 選用柔和乳黃與淡藍底色，減少刺眼強光，有效防範視覺疲勞與對比過敏。

🧩

Micro-Chunked Pacing 微型拆解低壓步調

Lessons are split into 5-minute daily micro-tasks without high-stress timers, accommodating executive dysfunction. 每天僅需 5 分鐘微學習，無倒數計時配備，減輕專注力負擔與考前焦慮。

🔊

Multimodal Audio Cues 多感官音效與視覺輔助

Combines text-to-speech audio with visual prompts to reinforce concept retention for auditory and kinesthetic learners. 結合語音朗讀與視覺提示雙重回饋，強化聽覺與觸覺型學習者的記憶留存。
