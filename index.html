LuminaBox — The Complete Bilingual Learning Kit & Digital Hub LuminaBox 光啟盒 — 雙語學習套裝與數位平台

LuminaBox is a bilingual learning kit and digital platform designed to help children gain confidence in English while staying connected to their schools, families, and communities.

Created by Bridge to English, a student-led charity with over 200 volunteers, LuminaBox makes learning fun, practical, and supportive. Families can use it at home with ease, while children enjoy gamified missions, rewards, and mentorship. LuminaBox（光啟盒）是一套雙語學習套裝及數位平台，旨在幫助兒童建立英語自信，同時加強與學校、家庭及社區的連繫。

由擁有超過 200 名志工的學生慈善機構 Bridge to English 研發，LuminaBox 讓學習變得有趣、實用且充滿支持。家長可以在家中輕鬆輔導，孩子則能在遊戲化任務、獎勵與導師陪伴中快樂成長。

Hands-On Learning 實體探索內容

Inside Your Physical LuminaBox LuminaBox 實體學習套裝內容

The physical kit provides tactile, screen-free engagement designed around four foundational pillars: Read, Speak, Write, and Connect. 實體套裝提供手感十足的非螢幕學習體驗，圍繞四大核心發展：閱讀、口語、寫作與社區連繫。

📖

1. Read / 閱讀

High-readability story cards featuring comic-style superhero visuals, designed specifically for early learners and SEN accessibility. 高可讀性圖文卡片，採用美漫風格超級英雄視覺設計，專為初學者與 SEN 學童研發。

🗣️

2. Speak / 口語

Interactive prompt cards that encourage children to introduce themselves, state their "superpowers", and express ideas confidently to family members. 互動提示卡鼓勵孩子主動介紹自己、分享「超能力」，並向家人自信地表達想法。

✍️

3. Write / 寫作

Guided activity sheets that make sentence building approachable through fill-in-the-blank puzzles and creative word webs. 引導式練習手冊，透過填空謎題與心智圖造句，讓寫作變得輕鬆簡單。

🤝

4. Connect / 連繫

"Community Helpers" and "Earth Heroes" missions that connect English learning to real-world social impact and neighborhood exploration. 「社區小幫手」與「地球英雄」任務，將英語學習連結真實世界與社區關懷。

Global Impact 永續發展目標

Rooted in Sustainable Development 響應聯合國永續發展目標

LuminaBox directly addresses educational inequality in local communities through strategic non-profit partnerships. LuminaBox 透過非營利夥伴關係，直接解決在地社區的教育資源差距。

Providing rich, multi-sensory bilingual learning materials paired with free 1-to-1 mentor support for every child. 為每位兒童提供多感官雙語學習教材及免費一對一志工輔導。

SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities

Subsidized at just HKD $30 per kit to ensure low-income families and SEN students access premium educational resources. 以每套 HKD $30 的資助價格，確保基層家庭與 SEN 學生享有優質教育。
