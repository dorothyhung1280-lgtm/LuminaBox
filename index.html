LuminaBox 光啟盒 LuminaBox 光啟盒 — 專為特殊學習需要（SEN）學生設計的雙語學習套裝

LuminaBox is an engaging bilingual learning kit and digital platform designed to help children build lasting confidence in English while staying connected to their schools, families, and communities. LuminaBox 光啟盒是一套結合實體教材與線上 AI 平台的雙語學習套裝，旨在協助兒童（特別是 SEN 讀寫障礙學生）建立英語自信，並深化與學校、家庭及社區的連結。

Created by Bridge to English, a student-led charity with over 200 dedicated volunteers, LuminaBox combines screen-free physical fun with interactive AI guidance to make learning enjoyable and stress-free. 本計畫由擁有超過 200 名熱心志願導師的學生慈善機構「Bridge to English」創立，將實體遊戲化工作簿與智能 AI 輔助相結合，讓學習變得無痛、輕鬆且充滿樂趣。

How LuminaBox Works LuminaBox 四步學習體驗

Follow these four simple steps to blend offline physical learning with online AI rewards! 只需跟隨以下簡單四個步驟，輕鬆結合實體紙本學習與線上 AI 獎勵機制！

1

Step 1: Do MC Physical Booklet 第一步：完成實體選擇題冊

Complete the hands-on multiple choice questions and activities in your physical LuminaBox workbook. 打開 LuminaBox 實體趣味練習冊，完成彩色高對比度且圖片豐富的英語選擇題。

📝
2

Step 2: Ask Volunteers for Help 第二步：線上尋求義工協助

Need help? Visit the website to connect with student volunteer mentors for free 1-to-1 guidance. 遇上不懂的題目？前往網站即可預約 Bridge to English 熱心大專學生義工，獲取免費一對一解答。

🙋‍♂️
3

Step 3: Enter Code in Portal 第三步：輸入解密碼進入 Portal

Enter the code from your Dial Cipher Card into LuminaPortal to unlock online games and verify progress. 將練習冊中解開的密碼輸入到 LuminaPortal AI 平台，解鎖互動 Arcade 遊戲與驗證進度。

🔑
4

Step 4: Collect Shards & Rewards 第四步：收集碎片升級或換禮物

Collect Lumina Shards to upgrade your AI companion avatar or redeem real physical gifts! 賺取 Lumina 光啟晶石碎片，用來升級專屬 AI 虛擬夥伴飾物，或換取真實實體禮品！

Dedicated Accessibility Suite 特殊學習需要（SEN）適應設計

Tailored SEN Learning Adaptations 專為讀寫障礙與 SEN 學生研發的學習功能

👁️

Lexend Dyslexic Font Lexend 讀寫友善字體

Specially expanded letter spacing and weight to avoid visual crowdedness and letter flipping. 加寬字距與特別字母間距設計，防止學生在閱讀時產生字母顛倒或視覺混亂。

📜

Soft Tint Screen Overlay 柔光防眩光色調

Replaces stark white backgrounds with warm pastel cream to reduce visual fatigue and scotopic sensitivity. 以柔和米黃與淡彩背景取代刺眼純白，大幅減輕視覺疲勞與光敏感不適。

🎧

Read-Aloud & Syllables 語音朗讀與音節拆解

Built-in text-to-speech engine breaks words down into manageable phonetic syllable chunks. 點擊即可發音，並自動將複雜單詞拆解為簡易音節，方便自然拼讀學習。

🎯

Low-Distraction UI 無干擾沉浸介面

Eliminates stressful countdown timers and cluttered menus to help students focus on one step at a time. 移除倒數計時器與複雜雜訊，提供簡潔高對比引導，減少學習焦慮感。
