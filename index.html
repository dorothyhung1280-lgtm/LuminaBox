LuminaBox — Monthly 4-Pillar Bilingual Learning Kit LuminaBox 光啟盒 — 每月四大核心雙語學習輔助套裝

LuminaBox is a monthly bilingual learning kit and digital platform designed to build English confidence for children through structured weekly goals. Each month delivers four distinct thematic booklets focusing on Speak, Read, Write, and Connect. LuminaBox 光啟盒是一套結合實體教材與線上 AI 平台的雙語學習套裝。每月按週規劃四本主題冊，分別著重於「口說 (Speak)」、「閱讀 (Read)」、「寫作 (Write)」與「社區連結 (Connect)」，協助兒童全方位建立英語自信。

Created by Bridge to English, a student-led charity with over 200 volunteers, LuminaBox makes learning structured, actionable, and engaging. Children complete daily exercises, sum scores on their Lumina Card to derive a 5-digit code, and log into LuminaPortal AI to earn Lumina Shards, upgrade companions, and notify dedicated volunteer mentors! 本計畫由擁有超過 200 名導師的學生慈善機構「Bridge to English」創立。學童完成工作天微任務計算出 5 位數「Lumina 密碼」，即可登入 LuminaPortal AI 賺取 Lumina Shards 晶石，升級 AI 寵物並向專屬導師發送輔導通知！

What's Inside Your LuminaBox? LuminaBox 光啟盒包含什麼？

📚

4 Thematic Booklets 4 本單週主題小冊

Booklets for Speak, Read, Write, and Connect, featuring dyslexia-friendly fonts and sensory-rich layouts. 針對「說、讀、寫、社區連結」設計的單週冊，採用讀寫障礙友善字體與多感官排版。

🔢

5-Digit Lumina Card 5 位數 Lumina 算式卡

Daily calculation card to log weekly progress, sum daily scores, and derive your Lumina Code for online rewards. 每日進度記錄卡，相加當天數值登入得出當週專屬解密金鑰。

🔔

Mentor Notification Pass 導師通知與預約卡

Submit requests using your unique Student ID (printed on the back of your box) to match with a mentor for flexible after-school/weekend support. 使用盒身背面的學生 ID 發送輔導請求，於課後或週末預約 1 小時專屬導師諮詢。

🧩

Tactile Accessories 觸感互動學習配件

Includes visual prompt cards, reward stickers, and community task badges for hands-on sensory learners. 包含視覺提示卡、獎勵貼紙與社區任務徽章，激發孩子觸感學習興趣。

Request Mentor Assistance 發送導師輔導請求

Need Extra Help with Your LuminaBox? 學習需要導師協助嗎？

Fill out the form below with the Student ID printed on the back of your box to notify a volunteer mentor! 請輸入盒身背面的 Student ID 並填寫表格，即刻發送輔導通知給志願導師！
