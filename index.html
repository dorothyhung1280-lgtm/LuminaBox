Bridge to English Support Project

Empowering Every Child with Accessible English Learning.

LuminaBox (啟迪語言連繫箱) provides primary school students—including neurodivergent and SEN learners—with structured, low-stress language tools. Created in collaboration with Bridge to English, our $20 physical box combines sensory cards with AI interactive games to build genuine confidence.
