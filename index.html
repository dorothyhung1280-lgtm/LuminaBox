LuminaBox 光啟盒 LuminaBox 光啟盒 — 專為特殊學習需要（SEN）學生設計的雙語學習套裝

LuminaBox is an engaging bilingual learning kit and digital platform designed to help children build lasting confidence in English while staying connected to their schools, families, and communities. LuminaBox 光啟盒是一套結合實體教材與線上 AI 平台的雙語學習套裝，旨在協助兒童（特別是 SEN 讀寫障礙學生）建立英語自信，並深化與學校、家庭及社區的連結。

Created by Bridge to English, a student-led charity with over 200 dedicated volunteers, LuminaBox combines screen-free physical fun with interactive AI guidance to make learning enjoyable and stress-free. 本計畫由擁有超過 200 名熱心志願導師的學生慈善機構「Bridge to English」創立，將實體遊戲化工作簿與智能 AI 輔助相結合，讓學習變得無痛、輕鬆且充滿樂趣。

Lexend Dyslexic Font Lexend 讀寫友善字體

Specially expanded letter spacing and weight to avoid visual crowdedness and letter flipping. 加寬字距與特別字母間距設計，防止學生在閱讀時產生字母顛倒或視覺混亂。

📜

Yellow SEN Background Mode 高對比暖黃背景模式

Replaces stark white backgrounds with warm high-contrast yellow tint to drastically reduce glare. 點擊即可切換至專屬暖黃底色高對比模式，大幅減輕視覺疲勞與光敏感不適。

🎧

Read-Aloud & Syllables 語音朗讀與音節拆解

Built-in text-to-speech engine breaks words down into manageable phonetic syllable chunks. 點擊即可發音，並自動將複雜單詞拆解為簡易音節，方便自然拼讀學習。

🎯

Low-Distraction UI 無干擾沉浸介面

Eliminates stressful countdown timers and cluttered menus to help students focus on one step at a time. 移除倒數計時器與複雜雜訊，提供簡潔高對比引導，減少學習焦慮感。
