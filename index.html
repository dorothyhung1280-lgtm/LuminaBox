LuminaBox 光啟盒

LuminaBox is a bilingual learning kit and digital platform designed to help children gain confidence in English while staying connected to their schools, families, and communities.

Created by Bridge to English, a student-led charity with over 200 volunteers, LuminaBox makes learning fun, practical, and supportive.

Step-By-Step Journey

How LuminaBox Works

Follow these four simple steps to blend offline physical learning with online AI rewards!

1

Step 1: Do MC Physical Booklet

Complete the hands-on multiple choice questions and activities in your physical LuminaBox workbook.

📝
2

Step 2: Ask Volunteers for Help

Need help? Visit the website to connect with student volunteer mentors for free 1-to-1 guidance.

🙋‍♂️
3

Step 3: Enter Code in Portal

Use your Dial Cipher Card code in LuminaPortal to unlock online games and verify progress.

🔑
4

Step 4: Earn Shards & Rewards

Collect Lumina Shards to upgrade your AI companion avatar or redeem real physical gifts!

Inside Your Physical LuminaBox

1. Read / 閱讀

High-readability story cards featuring superhero comic visuals.

2. Speak / 口語

Interactive prompt cards to state superpowers and build confidence.

3. Write / 寫作

Fill-in-the-blank puzzles and word webs for sentence building.

4. Connect / 連繫

Community missions linking English learning to real social impact.
