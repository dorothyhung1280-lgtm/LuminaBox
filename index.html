LuminaBox — Monthly 4-Pillar Bilingual Learning Kit LuminaBox 光啟盒 — 每月四大核心雙語學習輔助套裝

LuminaBox is a monthly bilingual learning kit and digital platform designed to build English confidence for children through structured weekly goals. Each month delivers four distinct thematic booklets focusing on Speak, Read, Write, and Connect. LuminaBox 光啟盒是一套結合實體教材與線上 AI 平台的雙語學習套裝。每月按週規劃四本主題冊，分別著重於「口說 (Speak)」、「閱讀 (Read)」、「寫作 (Write)」與「社區連結 (Connect)」，協助兒童全方位建立英語自信。

Created by Bridge to English, a student-led charity with over 200 volunteers, LuminaBox makes learning structured, actionable, and engaging. Children complete daily exercises across 5 weekdays, calculate a 5-digit Lumina Code, and enter it into LuminaPortal AI to interact with AI companions, solve homework questions, and play fun games! 本計畫由擁有超過 200 名導師的學生慈善機構「Bridge to English」創立。學童在每個工作天完成日常微任務，計算出的 5 位數「Lumina 密碼」，即可登入 LuminaPortal AI 解鎖 AI 夥伴、作業助手與互動遊戲！

How Your 5-Digit Lumina Code Works 5 位數 Lumina 密碼如何運作？

4 Easy Steps to Unlock Portal Magic! 4 個簡單步驟，輕鬆解密線上獎勵！

Follow this fun daily routine to calculate your unique weekly secret code. 照著以下日常步驟，輕鬆算屬於你的週秘密解密碼！

01 📝

Answer Daily Questions 完成每日微任務

Each weekday (Mon–Fri), answer the 3 multiple-choice questions in your weekly booklet. 每個工作天（週一至週五），回答週冊中的 3 道微型選擇題。

02 ➕

Sum Your Daily Numbers 相加當天的數值

Each answer gives a number (e.g. 3, -2, 1). Add all 3 numbers for that day to get 1 single digit (e.g. 3 + (-2) + 1 = 2). 選項對應數值（例如 3, -2, 1）。將當天 3 個數值相加算出 1 個數字（如：3 + (-2) + 1 = 2）。

03 🔢

Combine into 5 Digits 排列出 5 位數密碼

Line up your 5 daily results from Monday to Friday into a 5-digit code (e.g., 12345). 依次將週一至週五算出的 5 個數字排在一起，組合成 5 位數週密碼（如：12345）。

04 🎁

Enter Code & Claim Rewards 輸入密碼 • 解鎖獎勵

Enter your 5-digit code into LuminaPortal AI to feed your LuminaCompanion and unlock fun games! 將 5 位數密碼登入 LuminaPortal AI，解鎖 AI 夥伴升級與遊戲獎勵！
