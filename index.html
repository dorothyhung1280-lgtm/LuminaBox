LuminaBox (啟迪語言連繫箱) is Reinventing English education for underprivileged families and neurodivergent/SEN children across Hong Kong. For just HKD $20, LuminaBox pairs physical monthly activity cards with tailored, low-stress AI games and live volunteer tutoring—making language learning accessible, encouraging, and completely fun!

Structured Learning

Reinventing English in 4 Weekly Steps

Instead of overwhelming textbooks, students conquer one bite-sized target per week using simple yet powerful MCQ exercises.

W1

Week 1: SPEAK 🗣️

Phonics matching and AI voice coaching to practice speaking out loud with zero stress.

W2

Week 2: READ 📖

Decoding everyday signs and school notices into simple, digestible English chunks.

W3

Week 3: WRITE ✍️

Targeted MCQ vocabulary detective puzzles and grammar sentence building.

W4

Week 4: CONNECT 💬

Applying learning to real conversations with Bridge2English volunteers via live chat or Zoom.
